What is this?

Wonder Group doesn't have one central ERP, so a unified inventory ledger and purchase-order table in BigQuery act as the sub-ledger that Accounting uses to book the general ledger at month-end. Every morning a batch job validates the prior day's data, flags anything that looks wrong, and automatically opens a JIRA ticket routed to the right team or person. It tracks how long each ticket takes to resolve, spots errors that keep coming back, and auto-closes tickets once data engineering fixes the underlying table. This console is where staff across the company review those exceptions one at a time, and where managers keep an eye on turnaround.

✨ Good to know: auto-close

When data engineering fixes a table, the next morning's run simply won't find the problem anymore. Instead of asking someone to hunt down and close the old ticket, the system closes it for you and marks it Auto-Closed. You'll see these on the dashboard so nothing falls through the cracks.
